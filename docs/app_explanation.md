### InfusApp: An Introduction


### What is InfusApp
InfusApp is tool to record infusions performed by nurses.


### How InfusApp works
Once you are registered and logged in as a nurse, you are directly allowed to record infusions!
When recording an infusion, enter the patient's name and the medication/liquid you are going to infuse.
Press the button three time in the rhythm of the falling drops on your patient's plugged infusion.
The app will automatically record your infusion start as well as the calculated ending, which also helps you to know when to unplug the infusion.
Additionally, you can check all infusions that you have recorded so far.
